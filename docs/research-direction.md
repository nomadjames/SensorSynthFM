# Research Direction and Design Implications

SensorSynth FM is not trying to win by having the largest feature list.

Its distinctive design direction is passive sensing: treating the physical context around the performer as part of the instrument.

## What matters most
- passive sensing should be the identity of the project, not a side feature
- state legibility matters as much as sound design
- the first milestone should prove the thesis, not the full roadmap

## Competitive gap
Plenty of music tools give performers expressive control. Far fewer make the environment itself musically consequential.

That is where this project gets interesting.

## Design implication
The project does not need every possible mapping turned on at once. It needs a few mappings that are musically defensible, visibly legible, and strong enough to demonstrate why this instrument should exist.

## Better question
Not: how many features can fit in the instrument?

Better: what is the smallest prototype that makes the sensing thesis undeniable?
